The Opportunity

Your tribal data belongs on tribal land.

Every month, tribal nations pay external data center providers to store, backup, and manage tribal information — casino operations, government records, member data. That data lives on someone else's servers, in someone else's building, under someone else's rules.

Seebury works with tribal nations to change that equation. We design, build, and operate tribally-owned data centers on tribal land — moving existing co-location and disaster recovery contracts from external providers to facilities the tribe owns outright. The transition pays for itself: the monthly fees the tribe was paying go toward building an asset the tribe controls forever.

The data center becomes the anchor. From there, tribally-controlled energy infrastructure powers the facility and expands to serve surrounding tribal buildings, businesses, and members — creating a true tribal energy economy.

Why This Matters

The risks of data dependency

🔓

No Sovereign Control

Tribal data stored with outside vendors is subject to the vendor's terms, policies, and jurisdiction — not tribal sovereignty. In the event of a legal dispute, the tribe may not control access to its own records.

💸

Monthly Fees, No Asset

Co-location and cloud backup contracts generate zero equity for the tribe. Every payment builds someone else's infrastructure while tribal capital leaves the reservation permanently.

⚡

Grid Dependency

External data centers draw from regional power grids — subject to outages, rate increases, and carbon costs the tribe has no control over. A tribal facility powered by tribal energy eliminates this exposure entirely.

Power Strategy

100% uptime through a multiservice mosaic.

Data centers require power that never fails. The conventional approach — draw from the grid, back up with diesel — creates dependency, cost exposure, and environmental burden. Seebury designs a smarter solution: a "mosaic" of non-periodic energy sources that together deliver continuous, cost-competitive power at a fraction of the long-term grid cost.

Natural gas provides baseload reliability. Solar and wind reduce operating costs and earn green energy credits. Together they achieve near-continuous availability — while the tribe earns premium rates selling excess power back to the grid at peak demand times.

🔥

Natural Gas Baseload

On-site gas generation provides 24/7 reliable baseload power — mission-assured, independent of the regional grid, and capable of powering the full facility through any external disruption.

☀️

Solar Generation

Rooftop and ground-mount solar arrays reduce daytime gas consumption and operating costs. Tribal land tax advantages and federal incentives make solar economics especially favorable for tribal projects.

💨

Wind Integration

Where site conditions allow, wind generation provides renewable power when solar output is reduced — evening, cloudy periods, and seasonal variations — extending the coverage of renewable sources.

🔋

Battery Storage

A state-of-the-art battery storage system bridges the gaps between intermittent renewable sources and ensures a smooth, continuous flow of power regardless of generation variability.

3%

Annual Energy Cost Escalator Eliminated

Seebury uses a 3% per year estimated grid rate increase in financial modeling — tribal-owned generation eliminates this exposure entirely for the life of the facility.

Tribal Advantages

Why tribal land is the best location for this infrastructure.

Tribal nations are not just beneficiaries of the data center opportunity — they are structurally advantaged compared to commercial developers. Sovereign land, unique tax status, federal incentive programs, and tribal economic development tools create a financial and legal environment that no private developer can replicate.

Seebury's deep experience working with Native American tribes, First Nations, and indigenous communities means we understand how to structure projects that honor sovereignty, maximize tribal economic benefit, and meet the technical standards that major tenants and operators require.

🏛️

Sovereign Land Control

Tribal trust land is not subject to state or local tax. Data center infrastructure built on tribal land creates a unique cost advantage and jurisdictional protection that commercial sites cannot match.

💰

Tribal New Market Tax Credits

Tribes can leverage unique Tribal New Market Tax Credits, double depreciation rates, employment incentives, and other tribal-specific financial advantages that reduce project capital costs substantially.

⚡

Renewable Energy Incentives

Federal renewable energy tax incentives — including ITC credits applied to solar installations — offset approximately 50% of capital costs when properly structured, with unique tribal applications.

🌱

Green Energy Credits

Tribally-operated renewable energy generation qualifies for carbon credit programs and green energy premium rates — creating revenue streams above and beyond direct energy cost savings.

👥

Tribal Employment Priority

Data center and energy operations create high-tech, well-compensated career pathways — IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, energy management — that Seebury's training programs place tribal members into first.
